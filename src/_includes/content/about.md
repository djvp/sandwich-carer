The Sandwich Carer is an independent Australian publication about disability, ageing and mental health support. It's written for the people living it, and for the carers sandwiched between a complex system and the person they love.

The posts take what the system has just said and put it in plain English: what's useful, what to watch, and what got buried. The tools help with the big moments: guides, checklists, templates and scripts for everything from a plan review to an aged care assessment.
