THE ANIMATION, IN PRACTICE
Four reusable moves, not bespoke animation per post. Sam bobs on covers, side-eyes when jargon appears, squishes on hard-week posts, and the stamp slams on the payoff frame. The strikethrough draws itself across the jargon line.
Because Sam is five flat layers, each move is a preset that drops onto any post in minutes, and AI video tools can extend them without redrawing him.
What changed in v2. Sam redrawn as a bread slice (Susan's burger note), the 1c edge toolkit made explicit as four reusable moves, all five pillars from the brief shown, and the type specimen set at real sizes.
This is the sketch, not the final artwork. It becomes the brief for the web designer and creative: they redraw Sam as proper vector artwork with the mood set, profile crops and the four animation presets.
